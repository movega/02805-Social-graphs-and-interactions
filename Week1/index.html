The Marvel Popularity Trap

Does fame make a superhero central?

At first glance, the most connected superhero might seem like the most important one. But in a Wikipedia network, a link does not only tell us that two pages are related. It tells us which way attention flows.

A page can be heavily referenced by the rest of the network without referring out to many others. The same node can also be a hub of outgoing references without receiving the same attention back.
